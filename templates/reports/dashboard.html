Dataset Notes

Review before interpreting dashboard charts.

The dashboard summarizes historical damage and loss records. Some categories changed over time, so chart values should be interpreted with the dataset notes below.

Region and province coverage

Earlier reports may not have specified affected provinces, especially reports for infrastructure such as dams and irrigation systems.

Animal pest and diseases

The dataset does not include reports on animal pest and diseases such as ASF and AI.

2019 to present

Number of farmers/fisherfolk affected is available from 2019 to present.

2020 to present

There is limited to no submission/data of damage reports from BARMM.

2021 and earlier

Some values were lumped, including HVCC, Agricultural Infrastructure/Irrigation/Machineries/Equipment, and Fisheries and Aquatic Resources.

2022 to present

HVCC, AMEF, and Fisheries were further specified. Cassava, Fiber Crops, Coconut, Sugarcane, and Tobacco were specified separately and excluded from HVCC total.

2023 to present

Corn was further disaggregated into Yellow Corn and White Corn. Irrigation was further disaggregated into Small-scale Irrigation System and National Irrigation System.
